Live Operations

Status

Real-time reachability of the public Conxian service surfaces. Each surface is probed from the Conxian Labs edge on every refresh; a green state requires the expected service identity and a healthy response, but does not by itself establish entitlement or an SLA. Published libraries (Market, Enclave SDK, Corelibs) are not hosted runtimes and are not probed; their availability follows the platform.

Probing services…

Source: GET /api/status — the Conxian Labs site probes each service surface's public health endpoint, retrying up to three times within a 60s budget so a cold start is never reported as an outage, and caches the result for 30 seconds. Verified means the surface's own runtime answered with its expected identity. Unreachable means the runtime stayed unresponsive after every attempt. Safety mode means the runtime is up but has paused value-bearing operations. Published libraries (Market, Enclave SDK, Corelibs) are consumed by these services and are catalogued in GET /api/services; they are not separate hosts and are not probed here. Reachability is not a hosted-service, settlement or custody commitment.
