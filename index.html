The platform

Every layer, one console.

Six software surfaces across execution, verification, custody, and compute. Read the source, follow the documentation, or request the managed path.

GATEWAY

Managed gateway

ISO 20022 message translation middleware bridging Bitcoin and Stacks with legacy banking formats, with rate limiting, API-key auth, and audit logging.

Explore gateway
SDK

Enclave SDK

Cross-platform Rust/WASM builder surface for enclave, key-management, and signing boundaries. Hardware-provider enablement (Nitro, StrongBox, Secure Enclave) is a qualification target, not currently production-supported signing.

Explore SDK
WALLET

Conxius Wallet

Non-custodial Bitcoin and Stacks reference client; keys and signing stay on your device.

Explore wallet
NEXUS

Nexus node

State-observation and proof interface with REST and gRPC endpoints; SP1/RISC Zero remain research directions.

Explore nexus
MARKET

Market engine

Settlement and fee-model SDK under active development; liquidity routing, order matching, and metered billing are not yet live.

Explore market
CORE

Core library

Bitcoin cryptographic primitives, BitVM proof verification, and Taproot tooling.
