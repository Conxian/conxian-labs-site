The journey

Four steps, start to production.

Request your account

Console accounts are provisioned by the team while self-serve sign-up is being completed. Request one by email; no card required to explore.

Choose a plan

Start on Free or pick a managed tier. Managed plans are billed monthly; see pricing for units and terms.

Provision your service

Provision a managed gateway or connect your own node from the console. Keys are issued after payment settles.

Go live and monitor

Use the console and the public status page to watch your service. Cancel or change plans at any time.
