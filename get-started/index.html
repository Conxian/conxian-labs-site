The journey

Four steps, start to production.

Request your account

Console accounts are provisioned by the team while self-serve sign-up is being completed. Request one by email; no card required to explore.

Choose a plan

Start on the free open-source software or pick a managed tier where it is enabled. Managed access is packaging guidance rather than a live rate card; see pricing for units, terms, and boundaries.

Provision your service

Provision a managed gateway or connect your own node. Keys are issued after payment settles.

Go live and monitor

Use the console and the public status page to watch your service. Cancel or change plans at any time.
