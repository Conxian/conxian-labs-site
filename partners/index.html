Engineering and Delivery Partnerships

The Conxian Labs Partner Program is designed for specialized engineering firms, auditors, and solution providers who help clients evaluate and implement the public Conxian software surfaces. We provide technical context and commercial alignment for scoped integrations, pilots, and contract discussions without implying readiness beyond the evidence for a specific surface.

Ecosystem Support

Partners can align around the evidence, boundaries, and commercial scope needed for a specific client program.

  • Technical Alignment: Shared review of source, readiness material, integration assumptions, and open gates.
  • Joint Scoping: Collaborative commercial engagement for a named pilot or institutional program.
  • Evidence Context: Current capability and roadmap information shared when available and appropriate to the scope.

Commercial Boundaries

Conxian Labs provides the public software and evidence layer. Partners provide localized delivery and client-facing execution within the agreed boundary.

  • Commercial Brief: Four public surfaces and current buyer boundaries.
  • Scoped Pilot: Inputs, evidence checkpoints, and exit paths for a bounded evaluation.
  • Enterprise Path: Contract discovery for private deployment, support, or procurement requirements.
  • Packaging Guidance: Local, pilot, route-level, and contract mechanisms without invented rates.
