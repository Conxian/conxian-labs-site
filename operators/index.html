Infrastructure Baseline

Operators are expected to meet a minimum technical baseline to ensure ecosystem-wide reliability and security.

  • Secure Runtime: Hardened OS and enclave-capable hardware (StrongBox, SE).
  • Network Connectivity: High-bandwidth, low-latency connection to Bitcoin and Stacks endpoints.
  • Observability: Comprehensive logging and alerting aligned with institutional standards.

Operational Tracks

Choose the operational model that best aligns with your organizational capabilities.

  • Self-Hosted: Full control over the entire stack with internal engineering resources.
  • Managed Hybrid: Combining self-hosted components with Conxian Labs managed services where those are enabled and agreed.
  • Enterprise Path: For entities requiring full procurement-ready structure and SLAs.
