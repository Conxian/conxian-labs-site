Ecosystem Refinement & Governance Proposal

CXIP-1317 Status: Active Proposal & Architecture Specification Target: Org-Wide Protocol Upgrade & Refinement

Conxian Org-Wide Upgrade and Refinement Proposal (CXIP-1317)

CXIP-1317 codifies the structural transition from legacy monolithic architectures to a fully decoupled, high-performance Business-as-a-Platform (BaaP) ecosystem. It establishes formal rules for org-wide repository governance, security boundaries, and modular communication protocols across all active surfaces.

  • Monolith Purge & Decoupling: Complete retirement and org-wide purge of legacy monolith assets in favor of targeted domain micro-components.
  • Strict Domain Firewall: Strict operational separation between the open protocol surface (conxian.org) and corporate B2B services (conxian-labs.com).
  • Zero Secret Egress (ZSE): Enforcing compile-time secret isolation, immutable CI workflow scanning (Gitleaks), and fail-closed key management.
  • ISO 20022 Integration: Standardizing financial message transformation for seamless institutional settlement over Bitcoin L1 and Nakamoto Stacks L2.

Research Integrity & Evidence Tiers

Publication is not acceptance. Every claim on this surface is qualified by the strongest evidence that actually exists for it, so a reader can separate what is proposed from what is demonstrated. No tier is inferred from a repository existing or a build succeeding.

Proposed

A design direction, specification draft, or target. It describes intended behaviour only. Nothing at this tier may be read as deployed, audited, or available.

Implemented

Code exists on a branch and passes its own automated tests. This proves the code runs against those tests; it does not prove production deployment, provider acceptance, or third-party review.

Verified

Independently reproduced with a linked artifact: an external audit, a provider-side attestation, or an operation-specific acceptance record. Only this tier supports an operational or security claim.

Why the distinction matters

A green CI run, a merged pull request, and a healthy status probe each answer a narrower question than a security or settlement claim. Research here is written to make that gap explicit rather than to collapse it: a passing test harness qualifies the operations it exercises and nothing further. Where a claim cannot be reproduced, it is recorded as an open problem rather than asserted.

Supply-Chain & Dependency Security Posture

Sovereign infrastructure inherits the trust of everything it builds on. The ecosystem treats third-party dependencies as an attack surface with its own research and enforcement layer, applied across every active repository.

  • Dependency advisories: Rust crates are scanned against the RustSec advisory database, and JavaScript dependency trees against registry advisories. A finding is remediated by upgrading to a patched release or recorded with an explicit, reviewable exception — never silently suppressed.
  • Software bills of materials: Dependency graphs are generated as SBOMs so a consumer can inspect exactly what a build pulls in and evaluate its own exposure.
  • Secret egress scanning: Every change is scanned for credential material before it can land (Zero Secret Egress). A commit containing a secret is a build failure, not a review comment.
  • Static analysis: Multi-language CodeQL analysis runs on every pull request and on the default branch, with required checks gating promotion.
  • Pinned toolchains: Builds pin the runtime and compiler versions they are qualified against, so a "works locally" result and a CI result refer to the same toolchain.
  • Licence and provenance: Dependency licences and artifact provenance are reviewed as part of promotion, because an OSI-approved licence is a hard gate for downstream reuse and funding.

    • Dependency scanning reduces exposure; it does not establish that a system is free of vulnerabilities. A clean scan is evidence of the check having run, not proof of absence.

    Threat Model: Custody, Keys, and Replay

    The security research programme is organised around the boundaries that matter most: who can move value, where key material can exist, and how a message is proven fresh. Each boundary is studied as a set of failure modes, not as a single control.

    Custody boundary

    Conxian Labs is not a custodian and holds no client funds. The research interest is in keeping signing authority with the key holder, so a compromise of an operator service cannot itself move value.

    Key-material egress

    Non-custodial signing assumes private keys never leave their hardware boundary. The research asks what a remote service can learn and influence — nonces, message construction, policy — without ever receiving key material.

    Attestation and trust

    Hardware attestation (Android StrongBox, Apple Secure Enclave, cloud TEEs) is studied as a claim to be qualified per device and provider, including how attestation chains are validated and what happens when they cannot be.

    Replay and freshness

    Distributed settlement requires that a valid message cannot be reused. Research covers nonce, sequence, and idempotency design for value-bearing operations, and how retries interact with exactly-once expectations.

    Message integrity at the edge

    ISO 20022 translation sits between legacy banking rails and on-chain routing. The research surface is the transformation itself: schema fidelity, injection resistance, and where a translation error becomes a settlement error.

    Availability under degradation

    Free-tier and best-effort runtimes cold-start and throttle. Research treats the honest reporting of reachability, rate limiting, and identity as part of the security posture rather than a cosmetic concern.

    Open Problems & Research Roadmap

    Stating what is unsolved is part of the method. These are active research directions, listed as problems rather than as features, because none carries a verified solution today.

    • Universal proof verification: A production zkVM verifier that accepts proofs across prover backends without per-backend trust assumptions is not established. SP1 and RISC Zero remain directions, not a deployed universal verifier.
    • Oracle decentralisation measurement: How to measure and report the real independence of a proof-and-price oracle network — beyond node count — is an open question in the proof and price research track.
    • Cross-chain state finality: Reconciling Bitcoin L1 finality with L2 and external-chain state so a single settlement event is provably final, and not merely observed, remains unresolved.
    • Multi-cloud operational acceptance: Deployment scaffolding exists; accepted multi-cloud operations, high availability, and managed-node service levels are not established and are not claimed.
    • Attestation portability: A uniform way to validate device and provider attestation across Android, Apple, and cloud TEEs, and to fail safely where it cannot be validated, is still in design.
    • Privacy of settlement metadata: Reducing the observable metadata around ISO 20022 bridging and machine-to-machine settlement without weakening auditability is an open research track.

    Org-Wide Standards Execution & Rules

    All engineering and research activities across the Conxian organization adhere strictly to enforced operational rules and architectural standards.

  • Legacy Asset Governance: Legacy assets are permanently deprecated and purged across codebases, site surfaces, and documentation. All efforts focus exclusively on active modular components.
  • Domain Posture Compliance: The intended protocol and corporate domain map requires verified provider bindings, authentication/state checks and rollback evidence. Conflicting Labs/control ownership remains tracked under Platform #1167 and Business #1349.
  • Zero Secret Egress (ZSE) Enforcement: Strict .gitignore templates, automated secret scanning in GitHub Actions, and environment template whitelisting across all repositories.
  • Institutional UX Standards: Adherence to the "Ivory Foundation" design system with 17.5:1 contrast ratios, JetBrains Mono typography, 4px institutional borders, and zero-line tonal shifts.
