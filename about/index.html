Institutional Background

Conxian Labs

Conxian Labs is the builder and operator layer around the broader Conxian ecosystem. We are a South African engineering organization (CONXIAN LABS) focused on sovereign infrastructure, cryptographic primitives, and institutional-grade tooling for the Bitcoin and Stacks ecosystem.

Company details: CONXIAN LABS · Enterprise No. K2026884014 · South Africa.

Mission

We build, operate, and support the public surfaces that make the Conxian ecosystem understandable, usable, and easier to adopt — without replacing protocol ownership or custody.

Sovereign by Design

Every component we ship runs under the user's control. No custodial shortcuts, no hidden signing authority. The SDK, gateway, and enclave tooling are all built for self-sovereign operation.

Open Source First

Most infrastructure is MIT-licensed; Nexus is AGPL-3.0 and each repository's license is authoritative. Paid scope is limited to hosted operational services and enterprise support layers, never to the protocol boundary.

Institutional Engineering

We aim institutional engineering standards at the surfaces we operate — reproducibility, verification, and audit-ready documentation — and state per artifact what is proven versus still a target.

Ecosystem Portfolio

Conxian Labs maintains the following public infrastructure and tooling surfaces:

Conxius Enclave SDK

Rust-based cryptographic and enclave primitives with MuSig2, Schnorr, Taproot, and BitVM tooling. Hardware provider integration (Android StrongBox, Apple Secure Enclave, HSM) is a qualification target, not currently production-supported signing.

Conxius Wallet

Flagship non-custodial, local-first mobile reference client for sovereign key management.

Conxian Gateway

Rust-based gateway bridging application requests to protocol actions; a Beta integration surface for local and pilot-first evaluation.

Conxian Nexus

Source-available proof and state-observation surface (AGPL-3.0), deployed at nexus.conxian.org. Readiness is reported honestly on the status page.

Conxius Platform

Platform composition and lifecycle scaffolding for composing the stack locally and in managed environments; a contributor/operator surface, not an end-user product.

lib-conxian-core

Shared protocol and type boundary for the ecosystem, providing stable APIs and fail-closed verification boundaries. Not a paid tier.
